Healthcare · Clinical research · AI

AI Mind

A clinical decision support tool that helps researchers and clinicians spot people at high risk of dementia early.

RoleUX/UI Designer
Duration12 weeks
ScopeUser research, wireframes, visual design, prototyping, testing
8participants in usability tests
3+test sessions
3–4design iterations
90%task success on the final prototype

AI Mind aims to automate diagnostic screening with AI so healthcare professionals can intervene earlier. The tool assesses potential Alzheimer's patients from several data sources, initially for research, running on a central server.

The problem

Dementia is diagnosed too late to act on

Diagnosed late

Today's technology only diagnoses dementia once it has become apparent, often too late to start preventive therapy.

Treating symptoms

Treatment manages late symptoms, while patient investigations take time and predict risk poorly.

Costly screening

Screening for mild cognitive impairment (MCI) is complex, labour-intensive, costly and poorly predictive.

Goal: a high-fidelity prototype and a basic design system for phase one of AI Mind, helping clinical researchers and clinicians identify patients at high risk of dementia.

Assumptions

Better diagnoses

With a supportive decision-making tool, physicians can identify patients at high risk of developing dementia.

Richer signals

The finished tool assesses patients using MEG and EEG features, cognitive tests and genetic markers.

The users

A researcher and a clinician

HK

Dr. Helena Korhonen, 56

Clinical researcher in cognitive health at a university hospital, with expertise in EEG data processing.

Goals

  • Publish an article about her research
  • Explore which features can predict dementia
  • Test hypotheses at the group level and explore raw data
  • Control and understand every stage of the research

Frustrations

  • Raw EEG data is noisy and hard to clean automatically
  • Not enough freedom to explore data and train models
  • Results could become a black box she can't reuse
ML

Dr. Manuel Lopez, 49

Busy neurologist and researcher who sees AI's potential but values subjective factors in diagnosis.

Goals

  • Test how likely a specific patient is to develop dementia
  • Get a complete, understandable report to support diagnosis

Frustrations

  • Unclear who inputs data, and how and when
  • Worried the tool is too complex for clinical use
  • Needs to understand why the system reached a result
Journey

From setting up an analysis to publishing it

Start→Set the analysis→Run the test→See the result→Export for publication→Log out

Set the analysis

Define participant groups by demographics and time points, choose statistical methods and M/EEG features, confirm raw data is noise-free, then review and start.

Run the test

Check the expected run time, get feedback on progress, and do other work while it runs in the background.

See the result

Get notified, open the report, and review statistics, significant EEG features, accuracy and figures.

Bank User Agent

Redesigning a legacy virtual account product so corporates manage cash and payments with less effort.

Team4 designers

Redesignof a legacy product

Bank User Agent (BUA) is part of Virtual Account Management (VAM), a subset of Transactional Banking. It helps corporates manage their cash and payment transactions through virtual accounts. The goal was to give corporate banking clients more control over capital and a leaner, more efficient treasury.

Goals & objectives

VAM as a standalone product

Temenos wanted VAM to stand alone (it sat under Transact), with Direct Deposit Accounts, Liquidity Management and BUA running in parallel beneath it.

Improve the experience

Raise the overall user experience of a legacy tool.

Optimise interactions

Streamline interactions across the whole product.

Use the design system

Build with UUX components for easier development.

Stay consistent

Match the layouts of other products under the VAM umbrella.

User groups

A maker and a checker

M

Corporate Liquidity Manager

Also known as the Maker.

  • Gathers information from corporates to create records and requests for approval
  • Fast-paced; wants to input as much data as possible
C

Treasury Manager

Also known as the Checker.

  • Reviews the maker's requests to approve or reject them
  • Approval makes customer records, account details and structures live
Corporates use VAM through a self-service web portal on desktop, regularly, to service accounts, pull balances and transactions, and fetch account details.
Competitor analysis

Learning from the market leaders

OracleCiscoJP Morgan ChaseDeloitteGoldman Sachs

VAM isn't customer-facing software, so interfaces and documentation were hard to find. Oracle and Cisco were the only ones with public product documentation and screenshots, which let us study how they worked.

Tables over nav panes

Both relied on tabular layouts rather than a standard navigation pane.

Nested forms

Forms nested inside forms were common, and caused a lot of clutter.

Flat form models

Monotonous forms with no textual hierarchy.

Currencies and Maker-Checker

Currencies play a major role, and the Maker-Checker module is widely adopted.
